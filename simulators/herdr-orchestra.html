What the server is, in 3 lines

  1. herdr is like a building manager: the desktop app is the building, the server is the office that stays open in the background, and each pane is a room where one AI agent works.
  2. Closing the herdr window is fine. The office stays open, and the agents keep working. They stop only when you shut down the Mac.
  3. After a restart, open the herdr desktop app yourself and start your agents again. It is not a login item, so it will not open on its own.
